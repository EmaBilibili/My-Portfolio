# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and security teams evaluating Emanuel Binimelis for an entry-level SOC / Blue Team role. They need to quickly understand his current preparation, technical foundation, and the evidence behind his transition into cybersecurity.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Emanuel's transition from software development to cybersecurity. Its immediate goal is to support applications for a first security role by making the learning path, practical focus, and transferable engineering skills easy to evaluate.

## Positioning

An honest early-career security portfolio: it distinguishes active training from earned certifications and treats prior C# development work as technical evidence rather than as the main professional identity.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from job applications, recruiter searches, professional networking, or direct contact. They scan for a target role, active training, tools and areas of practice, technical evidence, and a contact path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Target path: SOC / Blue Team.
- Current training: TryHackMe SEC1, in progress.
- Planned next training: Hack The Box SOC Level 1.
- No cybersecurity certifications have been earned yet; the portfolio must not imply otherwise.
- Existing React portfolio supports Spanish and English, project history, experience, education, and contact.
- Retain a small amount of video-game/C# work only as transferable programming evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current training intent and roadmap supplied by the owner.
- Current C# / Unity background is represented by the verified evidence copy in `src/features/terminal/content.js`.
- No current cybersecurity projects, certifications, client work, or quantified security outcomes are available to claim.

## Product Principles

- State progress precisely; never inflate preparation into a credential or operational experience.
- Lead with defensive-security intent and learning momentum.
- Make transferable programming skills legible without competing with the security focus.
- Favor an evaluator's fast scan over decorative effects.
